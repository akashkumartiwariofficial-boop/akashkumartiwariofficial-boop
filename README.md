## `01 // ABOUT ME`

I am **Akash Tiwari**, a Computer Science student with a strong passion for **Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity**. My journey into technology is driven by curiosity, discipline, and a continuous desire to understand how systems work, how they can be improved, and how they can be secured in an increasingly digital world.

I actively engage with platforms such as **Hack The Box, TryHackMe, and GitHub**, where I sharpen my practical skills, explore real-world security challenges, and connect with a global community of learners and developers. I enjoy coding, solving complex problems, and participating in **Capture The Flag (CTF)** competitions to strengthen my analytical and problem-solving abilities.

I have hands-on experience with **C++ and Python**, along with practical exposure to **Linux environments**. I continue to develop my understanding of network security, vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, and secure system design. My interests lie in ethical hacking, responsible security research, and building resilient digital systems.

I am also the founder of a **student-driven cybersecurity initiative**, where I focus on identifying potential threats, analyzing risks, and exploring solutions that improve digital safety. Through this initiative, I aim to apply technical knowledge to practical scenarios while continuously expanding my cybersecurity expertise.

One of my strongest interests is the intersection of **AI and Cybersecurity**. I explore how artificial intelligence can support anomaly detection, threat identification, and more adaptive defense systems. My project interests include **AI-based anomaly detection and automated security workflows**, combining intelligent automation with practical security concepts.

Beyond technical skills, I value consistency, critical thinking, teamwork, and continuous self-improvement. My long-term goal is to become a leader in **AI-driven cybersecurity** and contribute to building secure, scalable, and intelligent systems that solve real-world problems.

I am currently seeking **internships, collaborations, open-source opportunities, and professional experiences** where I can apply my skills, learn from experienced professionals, and grow as a developer and security enthusiast. I am always open to connecting with like-minded people who share a passion for technology, innovation, AI, and cybersecurity.

<div align="center">

**Learn continuously. Think critically. Build intelligently. Secure responsibly.**
